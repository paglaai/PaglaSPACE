BRANDKIT · PAGLADESIGN

Represent PaglaAI, exactly

The BrandKit is the consumer surface of PaglaDESIGN — the single source of truth for mark, type, color, and usage. Monochrome first. Whitespace is the loudest element.

Brand

PaglaAI is a local-first AI ecosystem. Every product inherits the same mark, type, and values from PaglaDESIGN. The identity is intentionally restrained: monochrome first, typography-led, with color reserved for meaning only. Calm, precise, unmistakably human.

The tagline is Intelligence, Unhinged. Precise first, playful second.

Logo

The Pagla Face is the primary symbol. Single-weight, black-ink only. Never modified.

  • Black or white only.
  • Never stretch, distort, crop, outline, or rotate.
  • Clearspace equals sunglasses height; minimum 24px.
  • Wordmark never paired with the Face.

Usage guidelines

Do

  • Large open space and clearspace.
  • Black or white only, flat mark.
  • Whole mark on clean paper.

Don't

  • Stretch, alter, color, outline, rotate, or crop.
  • Effects, shadows, or glow.
  • Busy backgrounds or unauthorized elements.
