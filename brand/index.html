BRANDKIT

Represent PaglaAI, exactly

Monochrome first. The mark carries personality, the type carries precision, and whitespace is the loudest element.

Brand

PaglaAI is a local-first AI ecosystem. The identity is intentionally restrained: monochrome first, typography-led, with color reserved for meaning only. The brand should feel calm, precise, and unmistakably human.

The tagline is Intelligence, Unhinged. Precision, paired with a refusal to be contained. The voice is precise first, playful second — it does not shout, and it trusts the user to understand.

Logo

The Pagla Face is the primary symbol. A single-weight, black-ink only, minimal face — messy genius hair, calm sunglasses, a precise goatee. Flat, black on paper, never modified.

  • Black or white only. Never add colors, effects, shadows, or glow.
  • Never stretch, distort, crop, outline, or rotate the mark.
  • Clearspace equals the height of the sunglasses; minimum size 24 px.
  • The wordmark is never used together with the Pagla Face.

Colors

Two colors carry the brand: ink and paper. The accent is reserved for meaning only — a dot, an underline, or focus. It is never a background fill.

Usage guidelines

Do

  • Use a large open space and keep the clearspace.
  • Use black or white only, and use the mark flat.
  • Always use the whole mark, on clean paper.

Don't

  • Stretch, alter, color, outline, rotate, or crop the mark.
  • Add effects, shadows, or glow.
  • Use the mark over a busy image or add unauthorized elements.
