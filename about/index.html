ABOUT

Built openly, locally

PaglaAI is a local-first AI ecosystem — models, gateways, and launchers that respect your hardware and your data.

Mission

PaglaAI exists to make local AI the default. Cloud providers are useful, but they should never be a requirement. Every product in the ecosystem is built so you can run it on your own machine, keep your data local, and fall back to the cloud only when you choose to.

The ecosystem is one system across many surfaces: one design authority, one brand, one launcher, and a growing family of products that speak to each other openly.

The Architect

PaglaAI is the work of a single builder working openly — the alter ego of a human behind a mirror, keeping the whole system coherent. AYNAGHOR is the house of mirrors: a unified local intelligence that does not seek the answer to a question of man or machine, but of the mind.

  • One person owns the whole: launcher, products, and design authority.
  • Everything is committed in the open, decisions included.
  • The work is calm, precise, and unapologetically local.

Values

Local first

Your machine runs it. Your data stays on it.

Open

Source is public. Roadmaps are public. Reasoning is recorded.

Simple

One launcher, one design system, one family. Less to hold.
