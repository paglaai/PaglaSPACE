PAGLA · LOCAL AI, OPENLY BUILT

The local AI ecosystem

PaglaAI is a family of local-first tools — models, gateways, and launchers that run on your hardware and speak to each other openly.

One system across many surfaces

Every PaglaAI product inherits the same mark, type, and values. A design authority keeps the ecosystem consistent, and a shared launcher keeps it local. You never leave your machine to talk to it.

The ecosystem

  • PaglaMLX — LLM orchestration optimized for Apple Silicon, managed from the menu bar.
  • PaglaROUTER — a Cloudflare Worker gateway routing requests across providers.
  • PaglaDESIGN — the design authority: tokens, components, and brand, versioned.
  • PaglaCPP · PaglaAPI · PaglaGPT · PaglaOS — the roadmap beyond, in the same family.

How it is built

Local first

Your models, your data, your hardware. The cloud is a fallback, not a requirement.

Open, openly

Source is public, decisions are recorded, and the roadmap is on the page.

Simple and precise

Whitespace is the loudest element. Design communicates before it decorates.
