PaglaDESIGN v1.2 — the maturation pass

The maturation pass filled the system's structural layers. Interaction patterns landed as UX_PATTERNS.md — transitions, scroll rhythm, hover, loading, empty, error, and theme switching, every one token-referenced and gated by the motion and accessibility rules.

The component library grew to fourteen registered parts, each with real anatomy and behavior rather than a terse entry. Templates gave the site its page-level compositions — hero, landing, product, docs, blog, case-study, sections — so a page is assembled, not invented.

And the wireframes and mockups became token-referenced Markdown. They track the locked values exactly and never drift, instead of rotting the moment a color changes. The folders are no longer placeholders; they are a reviewable visual contract.
